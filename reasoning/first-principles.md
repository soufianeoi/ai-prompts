# First Principles Thinking

**Category:** reasoning
**Model:** Any LLM
**Use case:** Break down complex problems into fundamental truths and rebuild from there

## Prompt

Apply first principles thinking to [problem / challenge / goal].

**Step 1: Deconstruct**
Break the problem down into its most basic elements. What do we know to be true? What assumptions are we making? Separate facts from assumptions.

**Step 2: Question every assumption**
For each assumption, ask:
- Is this actually true?
- Could it be different?
- What would happen if this assumption were false?
- Who benefits from this assumption being accepted?

**Step 3: Rebuild from fundamentals**
Given what we know to be true (the first principles), what new solutions emerge? Don't be constrained by existing approaches.

**Step 4: Test**
How would you validate your new approach with minimal resources?

## Expected Output

A first-principles decomposition of the problem with assumption analysis and novel solutions.

## Tips

- Be ruthless about identifying assumptions
- Use the "5 Whys" technique to reach root causes
- Compare your rebuilt solution to the conventional approach
- Ask for a "minimum viable test" of your new idea
