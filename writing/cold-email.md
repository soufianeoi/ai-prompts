# Write a Cold Email

**Category:** writing
**Model:** Any LLM
**Use case:** Craft a professional cold email that gets replies

## Prompt

Write a cold email to [recipient role/title] at [company] about [purpose].

Context:
- I'm [your name/role] at [your company]
- We [what you do]
- I'm reaching out because [reason]
- Desired outcome: [schedule a call / partnership / feedback / etc.]

Tone: [professional / friendly / formal]
Length: Keep it under 150 words.

## Expected Output

A concise email with subject line, greeting, body, and clear call-to-action.

## Tips

- Personalize the first line (mention their work)
- Keep it to 3-4 sentences max
- Include a specific, low-friction ask
- Avoid attachments in the first email
