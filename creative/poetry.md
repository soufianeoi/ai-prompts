# Poetry Generation

**Category:** creative
**Model:** Claude / GPT-4 (low temperature for form, higher for free verse)
**Use case:** Generate poems in various forms and styles

## Prompt

Write a poem about [topic].

Form: [sonnet / haiku / free verse / limerick / villanelle / blank verse]
Tone: [melancholic / joyful / somber / playful / romantic]
Meter: [iambic pentameter / trochaic / none]
Rhyme scheme: [ABAB / AABB / free verse / specific pattern]
Length: [X lines or X stanzas]

Include:
- A strong opening image
- At least one metaphor or simile
- Sensory details (sight, sound, smell, touch, taste)
- A turn or shift in perspective (volta for sonnets)

## Expected Output

A complete poem following the requested form, with attention to meter and rhyme.

## Tips

- Provide a first line as a seed
- Mention poets you admire for style reference
- Ask for a revision pass focused on word choice
- Request an audio-optimized version (alliteration, assonance)
