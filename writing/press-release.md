# Write a Press Release

**Category:** writing
**Model:** Claude / GPT-4
**Use case:** Draft a professional press release for a product launch or company news

## Prompt

Write a press release for the following announcement:

Company: [company name]
Announcement: [product launch / funding / partnership / milestone]
Key details: [2-3 bullet points of important facts]
Date: [effective date]

Follow standard press release format:
1. FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
2. Headline (bold, attention-grabbing)
3. Subheadline (supporting detail)
4. Dateline (City, State — Date)
5. Lead paragraph (who, what, when, where, why)
6. Body (2-3 paragraphs with quotes, details)
7. Boilerplate (About [Company])
8. Media contact info
9. ### (end marker)

## Expected Output

A complete press release in standard format, ready for distribution.

## Tips

- Include a quote from the CEO or relevant leader
- Keep it to one page
- Avoid jargon and hype words
- Include link to more information or assets
