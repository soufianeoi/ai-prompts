# Ethical Dilemma Analysis

**Category:** reasoning
**Model:** Claude / GPT-4
**Use case:** Navigate complex ethical decisions using multiple frameworks

## Prompt

Analyze this ethical dilemma:

Situation: [describe the scenario]
Stakeholders: [who is affected]
My role: [your position / relationship to the dilemma]
Constraints: [legal, professional, personal boundaries]

Evaluate using these frameworks:
1. **Consequentialism** — what produces the best outcome for the most people?
2. **Deontology** — what duties or rules apply regardless of consequences?
3. **Virtue ethics** — what would a virtuous person do?
4. **Care ethics** — how does this affect relationships and responsibilities?

Then provide:
- Areas where the frameworks agree
- Areas where they conflict
- A recommended course of action with reasoning
- A "second opinion" counter-argument

## Expected Output

Multi-framework analysis with areas of consensus and conflict, plus a justified recommendation.

## Tips

- Include specific laws or regulations relevant to the situation
- Mention company policies or professional codes of conduct
- Ask for a "harm mitigation" plan for any choice
- Keep temperature low (0-0.3) for clear thinking
